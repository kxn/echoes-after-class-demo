# Footstep source

Kenney, **Impact Sounds 1.0**, Creative Commons Zero (CC0).
https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

`cloth_brick_0.wav` through `cloth_brick_2.wav` derive from
`footstep_concrete_000.ogg`, `002.ogg`, `004.ogg`. Band-limited, onset-trimmed,
shortened and softened for cloth/rubber-soled shoes on an old brick floor.
These are adapted hard-floor foley, not an authenticated historical shoe recording.

Original archive and license retained under `references/audio/`.

## School announcement
Original fictional school announcement synthesized locally using Windows Microsoft Huihui voice, then processed by `tools/process_school_broadcast.py`. Not an archival recording or a cloned voice. Dry segments and cue timing are retained under `assets/audio/broadcast/`.
